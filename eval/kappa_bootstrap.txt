KAPPA WITH UNCERTAINTY -- silver standard vs manual gold, 30 patients
==============================================================================

-- 1. point estimate -----------------------------------------------------
cells compared            : 2160   (both 1: 1080, both 0: 1070, silver 1/gold 0: 5, silver 0/gold 1: 5)
observed agreement p_o    : 0.9954   (2150/2160)
chance agreement p_e      : 0.5000
Cohen's kappa             : 0.9907

-- 2. is kappa distorted by label prevalence? -----------------------------
positive labels, gold     : 1085/2160 = 0.502
positive labels, silver   : 1085/2160 = 0.502
prevalence index |a-d|/n  : 0.0046
bias index |b-c|/n        : 0.0000
PABAK = 2*p_o - 1         : 0.9907
Labels are almost exactly balanced and the disagreements symmetric, so chance
agreement sits near 0.5, kappa and PABAK coincide, and neither prevalence nor
bias is inflating or deflating kappa.

-- 6. reading -----------------------------------------------------------------
With patients as the resampling unit, the 95% interval is 0.984 to 0.996.
Its lower end is above the conventional 0.80 threshold for almost perfect agreement,
so 30 patients are enough to support the claim that the silver standard agrees almost
perfectly with the manual labels. They are not enough to distinguish 0.99 from,
say, 0.98: that finer claim is not supported and should not be made.
None of this addresses the single annotator; that remains a limitation.
