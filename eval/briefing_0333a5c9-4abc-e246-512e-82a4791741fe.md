**1. Bottom line**
This is a chronic coronary disease patient with a history of MI, essential hypertension, type 2 diabetes, and severe obesity (BMI 40+), plus multiple comorbidities (CKD, prior renal transplant status, Alzheimer's). Highest-priority actions: optimize BP-lowering GDMT and add an SGLT2 inhibitor/GLP-1 RA for diabetes, and ensure cardiac rehab referral. Key safety concern: nitroglycerin spray must never be combined with a PDE5 inhibitor (risk of severe hypotension).
