# MAIN BRIEFING

**1. BOTTOM LINE**
This is a chronic coronary disease patient with a history of MI and CABG, essential hypertension, obesity, and ischemic heart disease, currently on beta-blocker, antiplatelet, statin, and RAAS-inhibitor therapy. The top priorities are cardiac rehabilitation referral, BP control to <130/<80, and weight-management counseling; the top safety concern is a cluster of current medications (ibuprofen, nitroglycerin spray, prasugrel, clopidogrel, metoprolol succinate) flagged for harm or lack of benefit in this population.

**2. SAFETY FLAGS (stop-and-check)**
- 24 HR metoprolol succinate (100 MG and 50 MG) -- no MACE benefit without prior MI/LVEF<=50% or other primary indication [BB:4, 3-NoBenefit]
- Clopidogrel -- not useful added to aspirin absent recent ACS/PCI indication [APT:7, 3-NoBenefit]
- Ibuprofen -- chronic NSAID use: increased CV and bleeding risk [APT:10, 3-Harm]
- Nitroglycerin spray -- do not combine with PDE5 inhibitors: severe hypotension risk [SHA:3, 3-Harm]
- Prasugrel -- avoid if history of stroke/TIA/ICH: fatal bleeding risk [APT:9, 3-Harm]

**3. ACTIONS FOR THIS PATIENT**
COR 1:
- Refer to cardiac rehabilitation [CR:1:1, COR 1]
- Target BP <130/<80 with lifestyle education [BPM:2, COR 1]
- First-line ACEi/ARB/beta-blocker (+ additional agents as needed) for BP control [BPM:3, COR 1]
- If on/requiring oral anticoagulation: DAPT 1-4 weeks then clopidogrel alone for 6 months (added to DOAC) [APT:11, COR 1]
- Weight-loss/lifestyle counseling for obesity [WM:2, COR 1]

COR 2a:
- If on/requiring oral anticoagulation and high thrombotic/low bleeding risk: continue aspirin + clopidogrel up to 1 month [APT:12, COR 2a]
- Consider GLP-1 receptor agonist (semaglutide preferred) for weight reduction [WM:3, COR 2a]
