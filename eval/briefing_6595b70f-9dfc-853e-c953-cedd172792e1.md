# MAIN BRIEFING

**1. Bottom line**
This is a CCD patient with a history of MI/ischemic heart disease, essential hypertension, type 2 diabetes (with diabetic kidney disease, CKD stage 3-4/ESRD, history of renal transplant/awaiting re-transplant), and severe obesity (BMI 40+). Priority actions: tighten BP control, start/confirm SGLT2i or GLP-1 RA for cardiovascular benefit, and refer to cardiac rehab and weight-management support. Key safety concern: nitroglycerin products on the med list carry a hard interaction warning with PDE5 inhibitors, and prasugrel carries a bleeding-risk warning; metoprolol and clopidogrel use should also be rechecked against indication.

**2. Safety flags (stop-and-check)**
- 24 HR metoprolol succinate 100 MG -- reassess ongoing indication; no MACE benefit without a primary indication for beta-blockade [BB:4, 3-NoBenefit]
- Clopidogrel 75 MG -- addition to aspirin not useful absent recent ACS/PCI-related DAPT indication [APT:7, 3-NoBenefit]

- Prasugrel 10 MG -- contraindicated with history of stroke/TIA/ICH: risk of significant/fatal bleeding [APT:9, 3-Harm]
